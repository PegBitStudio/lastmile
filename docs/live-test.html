Live voice test

Lastmile · 10 minutes

Nothing in this project has ever heard a real human voice. Everything is built and unit-tested, which is not the same thing.

Before you start

  1. Run npm run dev
  2. Driver screen: http://localhost:3000/drive
  3. Board, in a second window: http://localhost:3000/board

If the board says "No database on this deployment", stop. DATABASE_URL is missing from .env.local and nothing will save.

1The gate holds

Open /drive?speed=36

  • Red bar: "Waiting until you've stopped — 36 km/h"
  • A black SIMULATED SPEED tag
  • The button is grey and dead

Now change it to /drive?speed=0

  • Green bar, and the button turns black: "Report a drop"
2One sentence, one question

Stay on ?speed=0. Leave Nigeria / Lagos selected, tap the first stop, press Report a drop, and wait for "Listening. Go ahead."

Then speak normally. Do not slow down for the machine.

Customer wasn't in, I left it with the gateman at the side gate.

  • Your words appear on screen as you speak
  • The record fills in: outcome, who took it, entrance
  • It asks one question, and it is about the name
  • It does not ask what happened, where, or which stop

His name is Musa.

  • It reads the record back once
  • It stops listening by itself — you never press Stop
  • Within 2 seconds the record is on the board, tagged closed
This is the one that matters. If it asks about something you already said, the missing-field list is not reaching it. That is the whole product failing, and I need to know at once.
3The board tells the truth

Find that record on the board.

  • Stated shows your words
  • Observed shows stationary and the time — unknown is correct on a laptop
  • Proof says "not captured in this build"
  • A hear button sits beside the name. Press it: you should hear yourself
Wait about 10 seconds and look again. If the second model was unsure of "Musa", the card gets a red NEEDS REVIEW tag and the name is underlined. Either result is fine — just tell me which one you got.
4A different country

Back on the driver screen, tap Pakistan / Lahore. The route changes to the Lahore stops. Tap one and press Report a drop.

Nobody was home, gave it to the chowkidar at the main gate. His name is Bilal.

  • "chowkidar" is transcribed correctly — not "chalk a dar"
  • The record says the relationship is security
  • Nothing else is asked, because you gave everything

This is the region pack earning its keep, and it is the strongest twenty seconds of the video.

5Cash

Tap Nigeria / Lagos, then the stop marked COLLECT 18,500.

Delivered to the customer, Folake. She only had ten thousand, paid cash.

  • It asks what happens next, and nothing else
  • It does not ask why the money was short — it already knows
  • The board shows collected 10000 of 18500 — short
6The phone

Test 2 again — on your phone, on mobile data, on the Vercel URL. Allow the microphone and the location when asked.

  • The agent is audible through the phone speaker
  • The gate shows a real speed rather than unknown

If something goes wrong

Send all three. Any one alone is usually not enough.

  • What you said, and what it said back — copy the lines off the screen
  • The browser console — press F12, click Console, copy anything red
  • The terminal running npm run dev — the last 20 lines

The most useful sentence you can send is "it asked me X after I had already said Y", because that names the failure exactly.

If tests 1 to 3 pass, the demo can be filmed. If test 2 fails, nothing else matters until it is fixed.
